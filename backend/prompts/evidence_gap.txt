EVIDENCE GAP IDENTIFICATION INSTRUCTIONS

When identifying evidence gaps, look specifically for:

1. MISSING CLINICAL DATA
   - No human clinical trial data available
   - Evidence exists only from preclinical (in vitro / animal) studies
   - No Phase II or Phase III trials completed
   - No randomized controlled trial data

2. POPULATION GAPS
   - Studies limited to a specific demographic (e.g., adult males only)
   - Lack of data in pediatric, elderly, or diverse ethnic populations
   - No data in patients with comorbidities common to the target condition

3. COMPARATIVE EFFECTIVENESS
   - No head-to-head comparisons with existing standard-of-care treatments
   - Only placebo-controlled data, no active comparator trials
   - Long-term comparative outcomes not studied

4. MECHANISTIC GAPS
   - Mechanism of action proposed but not confirmed in humans
   - Target engagement demonstrated in vitro but not in vivo
   - Biomarker for response not identified or validated

5. SAFETY GAPS
   - Long-term safety data unavailable
   - Drug-drug interaction studies not conducted
   - No data on reproductive toxicity or special populations

6. REGULATORY GAPS
   - Not approved for the indication under study
   - Orphan drug designation not pursued despite rare disease context
   - Bioequivalence or biosimilarity data absent

Present identified gaps factually and concisely. Do not speculate beyond what the absence of evidence implies.
